Ride the tide.

Point any OpenAI client at Tide. Your request is routed to whichever nodes are free, sharded across GPUs if the model is too big for one, and settled on-chain.

  • Uncensored open models. Only illegal content is filtered.
  • Nothing stored. Prompts and outputs never touch our database.
  • Receipts. Verify which nodes served your tokens.
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    base_url="https://api.tide.network/v1",
    api_key="tide_sk_...",
)

res = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="deepseek-v4-flash",
    messages=[{"role": "user",
               "content": "Explain the tides."}],
    stream=True,
)
# ≈ served by 6 GPUs across 4 countries
